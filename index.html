About

Enjoy Solutions is a one-person software company in Gothenburg, Sweden, run by Richard Marklund. Before starting Enjoy Solutions, Richard spent 10+ years building backend services for high-traffic systems in the automotive industry.

Now he's building Oscar — an AI agent for iPhone.

Oscar

Oscar is an AI agent for iPhone that goes beyond chat. It browses the web, saves notes, manages your reminders, syncs with your calendar and email — and keeps learning about you over time.

  • Web browsing — research, monitor listings, follow your favourite sports teams, and more
  • Smart reminders — recurring notifications and scheduled follow-ups
  • Cutting-edge memory — Oscar remembers context across conversations so you never repeat yourself
  • Calendar integration — syncs with your calendar to plan, schedule, and stay on top of your day
  • Email integration — draft, summarize, and manage emails directly through Oscar
  • Notes and recall — save anything and retrieve it instantly when you need it
