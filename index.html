About

Enjoy Solutions AB is a software company based in Gothenburg, Sweden, founded by Richard Marklund. With over a decade of experience engineering backend services for high-traffic systems in the automotive industry, Richard started Enjoy Solutions to build products that bring the same reliability and performance to consumer software.

Today the company develops Oscar, an AI-powered personal assistant for iPhone, and provides software engineering consultancy to businesses looking to build robust, scalable systems.

Oscar

Oscar is an AI-powered personal assistant app for iPhone that automates the small tasks that fill your day. From monitoring apartment listings and researching purchases, to adjusting recipes, creating study plans, and setting smart reminders — Oscar handles the details so you don't have to.

  • Automated monitoring — scans websites daily and notifies you when matches appear
  • Photo analysis — identify plant problems, scan documents, and more
  • Smart reminders — recurring notifications and scheduled follow-ups
  • Free to download — no credit card required to get started

Services
