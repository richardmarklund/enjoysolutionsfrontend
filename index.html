Ripsa

Note-taking support for higher education.

Ripsa records lectures, transcribes them and lets students search, summarize and ask questions about the content. It is sold to universities and colleges, who manage it through an admin portal.

  • Live transcription Lectures become searchable text in real time.
  • Summaries Key concepts pulled out of every lecture.
  • Ask questions Answers grounded in what was actually said.
  • Private by design Data stored and processed in the EU by European companies. GDPR compliant.
